# Lumeo NumberInput adaptation

`SetupNumberInput.razor` adapts layout, theme/density integration and accessibility conventions
from Lumeo 5.9.1, `src/Lumeo/UI/NumberInput/NumberInput.razor`, commit
`6b82fb32a8a191a69469180590fde00cd17b4753` at https://github.com/Brain2k-0005/Lumeo.
Darkspyre owns the typed parsing, validation and input-state integration. Lumeo is not forked or modified.
